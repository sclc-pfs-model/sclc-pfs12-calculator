For research use only. The model is prognostic. It must not be used to select or withhold immunotherapy, and it does not replace clinical judgement.

Who it applies to

Adults with stage IV (M1) small-cell lung cancer starting first-line platinum–etoposide with or without a PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor. The estimate is made before treatment starts.

How the estimate is calculated

Predicted 12-month PFS = S0exp(Σβ(x − x̄)), with a separate baseline S0 for each treatment.

  • S0(12 months) = 0.0896 for chemotherapy and 0.1871 for chemo-immunotherapy.
  • LDH below 50 U/L is treated as 50 U/L. If LDH is not available, ln(LDH/250) is set to −0.0463.
  • Risk groups use fixed thresholds of predicted 12-month PFS: low risk ≥25%, intermediate risk 10% to <25%, high risk <10%.

Evidence and limits

The model was developed in 365 patients at one centre in China and validated in a later cohort from the same centre (203 patients) and in a small cohort from a second centre (61 patients). LDH was measured by the IFCC lactate-to-pyruvate method (reference interval 120–250 U/L); values from other assays may not be comparable. Larger external validation is needed before use at other centres.
